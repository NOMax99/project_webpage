Our Team

An international network of experts in sustainable agriculture

Project Management

The DisCoverCrop project is led by an experienced team of leading scientists who bring their expertise in agroecology, soil sciences, and sustainable agriculture.

NORSØK Team

Coordination & Project Management

The Norwegian team coordinates the overall project and conducts field trials in Scandinavian climate zones.

SLU Team

Soil Ecology & Biodiversity

Experts from the Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences focus on soil biodiversity and ecological interactions.

WUR Team

Plant Physiology & Yield Optimization

The Dutch team investigates physiological aspects and yield effects of different cover crop strategies.

FZ Jülich Team

Soil Sciences & Modeling

The German research team specializes in soil chemistry and modeling of nutrient cycles.

EMU Team

Sustainable Land Use

The Estonian team brings expertise in sustainable land use and climate adaptation.

Research Groups

Soil Ecology Group

This interdisciplinary group studies microbial communities in soil, nutrient cycles, and the effects of different cover crop strategies on soil fertility. The team uses state-of-the-art molecular biological methods and long-term monitoring.

Plant Research Group

Focus on selecting and evaluating optimal cover crop mixtures for different cropping systems and climate zones. The team conducts extensive field trials and develops practical recommendations.

Biodiversity Group

Monitoring the impacts on biodiversity above and below ground. The group works closely with ecologists to assess effects on insects, birds, and soil fauna.

Modeling Group

Development and application of computer models to predict long-term effects and optimize management strategies under different climate scenarios.

PhD Students & Postdocs

The project is supported by several early-career researchers who work on various aspects of cover crop cultivation as part of their doctoral or postdoctoral research. These young researchers bring fresh perspectives and innovative approaches to the project.

Technical Staff

A dedicated team of laboratory technicians, field assistants, and data analysts ensures the smooth conduct of experiments and the quality of collected data.
