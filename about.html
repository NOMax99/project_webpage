About DisCoverCrop

An international research project developing sustainable cover crop strategies

The Project

DisCoverCrop is an ambitious research project dedicated to exploring and promoting sustainable cover crop and green manure strategies. Our goal is to improve soil fertility, increase yields, and enhance biodiversity in agricultural systems.

Our Mission

We investigate the effects of different cover crop mixtures and management strategies on:

  • Soil fertility and soil health
  • Yield potential of main crops
  • Biodiversity in agricultural ecosystems
  • Sustainability of agricultural practices
  • Climate resilience in agriculture

International Collaboration

DisCoverCrop is a collaboration of leading European research institutions:

SLU

Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences – Expertise in sustainable agriculture and soil ecology

WUR

Wageningen University & Research – Leading in agricultural sciences and plant research

FZ Jülich

Forschungszentrum Jülich – Specialized in agroecology and soil sciences

NORSØK

Norwegian Centre for Organic Agriculture – Project coordinator

EMU

Estonian University of Life Sciences – Expertise in sustainable land use

Research Approach

Our project combines field trials, laboratory analyses, and modeling to gain comprehensive insights into the effects of cover crops. We work closely with farmers to develop practical solutions applicable in different European climate zones.
