Project Objectives

Our research focus and methodological approaches

Main Objectives

The DisCoverCrop project pursues several ambitious research objectives to improve the sustainability and productivity of agriculture.

Soil Fertility

Investigation of the effects of different cover crop mixtures on soil quality, nutrient availability, and microbial activity.

Yield Optimization

Assessment of the influence of cover crops on the yields of subsequent main crops in different cropping systems.

Biodiversity

Monitoring the impacts on species diversity, particularly in soil organisms, insects, and farmland birds.

Climate Resilience

Development of strategies to adapt to climate change through improved water storage and soil structure.

Nutrient Management

Optimization of nitrogen fixation and reduction of nutrient losses through targeted cover crop selection.

Practice Transfer

Development of practical recommendations for farmers to implement sustainable cover crop strategies.

Research Methods

Our multidisciplinary approach combines various scientific methods:

Field Trials

  • Long-term trials at multiple sites across Europe
  • Different cover crop mixtures under real-world conditions
  • Comparison of conventional and organic farming methods
  • Monitoring over multiple growing seasons

Laboratory Analyses

  • Soil chemical and physical analyses
  • Microbiological studies of soil biota
  • Nutrient analyses in plants and soil
  • DNA sequencing for biodiversity assessment

Modeling

  • Computer models to predict yield effects
  • Simulation of nutrient cycles
  • Climate change scenarios and adaptation strategies
  • Economic evaluation of different strategies

Experimental Design

The field trials are conducted at five sites in different European climate zones. Each site tests multiple cover crop mixtures with different species compositions and management strategies. Data are continuously collected and shared among partners to enable comprehensive analysis.
