Research Results

Current findings from our field trials and analyses

Key Findings

The preliminary results of the DisCoverCrop project show promising trends for sustainable cover crop management.

Soil Fertility +15%

Significant improvement in soil quality through diverse cover crop mixtures compared to monocultures.

Yield Increase

Average 8-12% higher yields in main crops following optimized cover crop mixtures.

Biodiversity

Increase in species diversity of soil organisms by up to 30% in plots with diverse cover crops.

Water Storage

Improved water storage capacity of soil through increased humus content and better soil structure.

Nitrogen Fixation

Legume-rich mixtures fix up to 150 kg N/ha and reduce the need for mineral fertilizers.

Weed Suppression

Natural weed suppression through dense cover crop stands, reduction of herbicide use possible.

Ongoing Studies

Our research is a continuous process. We are currently focusing on the following areas:

Long-term Effects

Monitoring the long-term impacts of different cover crop strategies over multiple crop rotation cycles. Initial results suggest cumulative positive effects that increase over several years.

Climate Adaptation

Assessment of the resilience of different cover crop mixtures under drought stress and extreme weather conditions. Mixtures with deep-rooting species are particularly promising.

Economic Evaluation

Cost-benefit analyses for different management strategies show that investment in cover crops pays off after 2-3 years.

Microbiome Analyses

DNA sequencing shows that diverse cover crop mixtures promote a richer and more functional soil microbiome, which is crucial for soil health.
